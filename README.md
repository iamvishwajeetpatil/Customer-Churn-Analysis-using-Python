# 📊 Key Business Insights

## 📌 Overall Churn Rate
- Approximately **26.5% customers churned**
- Around **73.5% customers were retained**

This indicates the company has a stable customer base but still faces significant customer loss.

---

## 🌐 Internet Service Analysis
- Customers using **Fiber Optic Internet** showed the highest churn rate.
- DSL users had lower churn.
- Customers without internet service had the lowest churn.

✅ Insight:
Fiber optic customers may be facing pricing or service quality issues.

---

## 🔒 Value Added Services Impact

Customers without the following services had significantly higher churn:
- Online Security
- Tech Support
- Device Protection
- Online Backup

✅ Insight:
Customers using additional support/security services are more likely to stay loyal.

---

## 📅 Contract Type Analysis
- Customers with **Month-to-Month contracts** showed maximum churn.
- Long-term contract users had much lower churn rates.

✅ Insight:
Long-term subscriptions improve customer retention.

---

## 💳 Payment Method Analysis
- Customers using **Electronic Check** had higher churn behavior.
- Automatic payment users were more stable.

✅ Insight:
Payment experience may influence customer satisfaction.

---

## 👴 Senior Citizen Analysis
- Senior citizens showed slightly higher churn rates compared to non-senior customers.
